Harbor Deck browser UI

Road: equipped gun, selected form, attachment slot and current goal. No stat upgrades.
Gear: six guns, per-gun attachment fitting and melee form selection.
Build: nine stat upgrades, Overclock, weapon mastery and form specialization.
Voyage: campaigns, expeditions and district travel.
Harbor: restoration and contracts.
Journal and Settings are direct buttons in the battle margin.

The battle canvas and its camera transform do not change when selecting a page.
The shared action rail contains farming/frontier travel, volley and form activation.
Only the lower page scrolls. Navigation and currency balances remain fixed.
On tall phones, the battle ends at approximately 54% of the viewport; it is
identical across all destinations. Existing shorter-view sizing remains supported.
Old reward and training links route to the appropriate new destination.
Existing save format and unlock rules are retained. No currency reset or new purchases.

Gun assets
Six approved Harbor Armory designs were converted to a transparent sprite atlas
using built-in image generation (no GodModeAI use). Sprite slicing preserves alpha.
Individual sprites: web/weapons/*-comic.png. Grip, split and muzzle coordinates:
web/weapon-art.js. Existing gun mechanics and effects are unchanged.
