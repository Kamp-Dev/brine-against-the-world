Endless upgrade balance — October 6, 2026

Core and advanced upgrades have no designed rank ceiling except Deadeye (100 ranks,
200% chance) and Rupture (30 ranks, 300% crit damage). Overclock ranks are unlimited.
Weapon attachment slots, building restoration tiers and campaign tiers remain discrete
unlock systems, not repeatable stat ranks.

Crit tiers: at 150%, every hit crits and half receive a second tier. Each tier adds
(crit multiplier minus 1) to normal damage. At 200% chance / 300% damage every hit is
5x, not 9x. This keeps double-tier crits strong without exponential multiplication.

Early prices stay unchanged through rank 30. Thereafter costs scale as rank^1.5,
replacing exponential prices. Existing linear enemy health/damage and stage rewards
continue beyond the former save limit of 10,000 stages. Existing rank and currency
saves are preserved. Currency no longer stops at one billion or wraps at 32 bits.

Speed gains taper after rank 30. Visible fire rate stops at 12.5 shots/sec; further
power is transferred to shot damage so it still increases DPS without overwhelming
animation/projectile counts. Armor scales as rank/(80+rank). Recovery and tide charge
approach 100% with diminishing returns. These have no purchase cap.

Infinite-rank bars show progress toward each next 10-rank milestone. Number formatting
uses compact suffixes and scientific notation for extremely large values. JavaScript
safe integer precision still bounds ranks/stages; this is not arbitrary precision.

Validation: all 22 suites, including new purchases/gains through rank 100,000,
stage 1,000,000 persistence, Overclock past 100, caps and double-tier crit output.
Browser verified an actual rank-101 purchase with an isolated test save. This is a
browser balance update; the separate Unity implementation is not synchronized here.
