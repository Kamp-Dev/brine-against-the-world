Scrapyard Shop
Available in Gear, with shell paints, weapon casing paints and one mod per gun.
Purchases use salvage, have fixed prices and progression gates, and persist in saves.
Existing saves receive an empty shop without losing progression.

Projectile artwork: original eight-frame sheets supplied by the user. Frames use alpha bounds; the source PNGs are unchanged. Brick and duck tumble; cushion ping-pongs with squash and bob; confetti uses colorful canvas particles. Projectiles remember the equipped mod at firing time.

Comic captions: !!, Quack, Pfft (wind emoji). Eligible direct hits roll a 30% chance after at least ten real seconds, followed by another 10-14 second cooldown. Game speed does not accelerate the cooldown. Captions last 1.3 seconds and do not pause combat.

Paint uses a saturated rust/orange mask, preserving alpha, cream and dark ink. Recolored frame cache is bounded to 48 MiB. Original artwork is unchanged.

Mods: brick +25% damage with 20% longer shot interval; cushion fourth hit +45%; duck fifth hit heals 2% max HP; confetti -10% damage and +15% salvage on gun-finished road battles, excluding campaign rewards.

Ships/passive income remain proposed concepts, not part of this release.
